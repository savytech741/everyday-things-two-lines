Two lines that explain a toaster to a 10-year-old: line 1 uses a sunbed analogy to make the glowing wires and the pop-up vivid, and line 2 says honestly what the analogy maps — lying flat under glowing heat to turn brown — and what it leaves out — bread has no choice and no sunscreen, so too long means burnt, not tanned. An entry for the make.fun bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run".
